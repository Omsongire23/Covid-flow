Why Get Vaccinated?

COVID-19 vaccines train your immune system to identify SARS-CoV-2 spike proteins without causing the disease itself. Real-world surveillance demonstrates over 90% reduction in hospitalization and death among fully vaccinated and boosted individuals.

  • Protection: Lowers the severity of infection significantly.
  • Variant Defense: Boosters broaden neutralising antibody titers against emerging variants.
  • Community Immunity: Lowers transmission vulnerability in elderly and immunocompromised individuals.

Safety & Monitoring

Billions of vaccine doses have been administered globally under the most intensive safety surveillance monitoring history.

Common Mild Side Effects: Tenderness at injection site, low-grade fever, fatigue, or muscle soreness resolving in 24–48 hours.
