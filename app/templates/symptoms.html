What To Do If You Feel Sick

Stay home and isolate in a well-ventilated room away from family members. Monitor blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) with a pulse oximeter — seek emergency care if it drops below 94%. Consult a qualified medical professional for diagnosis and clinical treatment guidance.
