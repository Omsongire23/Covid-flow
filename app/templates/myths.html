MYTH

Drinking hot water, alcohol, or herbal tea cures COVID-19.

FACT

No beverage or home remedy eliminates SARS-CoV-2 from your respiratory tract. Only approved antiviral medications prescribed by doctors and clinical supportive care are proven effective.

Authority: World Health Organization (WHO) Mythbusters
MYTH

5G mobile networks transmit the virus.

FACT

Viruses cannot travel on radio waves or mobile networks. COVID-19 spreads via respiratory droplets emitted when an infected person coughs, sneezes, or speaks.

Authority: WHO & International Telecommunication Union (ITU)
